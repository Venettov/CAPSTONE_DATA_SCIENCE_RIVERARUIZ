Understanding Social Vulnerability Indices

01 Indicator Methodology

Social vulnerability refers to the resilience of communities when confronted by external stresses. The indicators shown here—ranging from economic strain (`pct_below_poverty`) to demographic factors (`pct_aged_65_plus`)—provide a comprehensive view of which areas require prioritized resource allocation.

02 The 2010–2024 Trajectory

Puerto Rico's trajectory has been marked by extreme external shocks, including debt crises and catastrophic hurricanes. This dashboard visualizes how these events have shifted the vulnerability landscape, with many rural interior municipalities seeing sustained increases in social risk scores.
