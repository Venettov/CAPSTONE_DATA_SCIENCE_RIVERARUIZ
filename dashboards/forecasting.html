Dashboard Interpretation

Use this dashboard to review the Phase 4 artifact system: which tables were produced, which figures are available, and whether the policy analysis is connected to the intended model backbone.

The most useful figures for the final report are likely the island policy ranking, retained-residents ranking, regional policy comparison, regional heatmap, recovery-rebound endpoint comparison, and recovery-rebound sensitivity paths.

The most useful tables for appendix review are likely the executive summary table, island policy summary, region policy summary, best package by region, recovery endpoint uncertainty table, and policy mechanism table.
